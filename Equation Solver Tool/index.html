Equation Solver Tool

Enter a single-variable algebraic equation (e.g. 2x + 3 = 7 or x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0), or a system of linear equations separated by ; (e.g. 2x + y = 5; x - y = 1). The tool will produce a step-by-step breakdown and the result. Export results as text when done.
